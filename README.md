## 🤖 About AeroNova

AeroNova is a feature-rich Discord music bot serving **10,000+ users** across **60+ servers**.

**Features:**
- 🎵 Play music from YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud & Apple Music
- 🎛️ Filters — Bassboost, Nightcore, 8D, Speed, Pitch & more
- 📋 Playlist & queue management
- 🔁 Loop, shuffle, autoplay
- 🎚️ DJ role system
- ⚙️ Per-server setup panel
- 👑 Premium plans (Basic / Pro)
